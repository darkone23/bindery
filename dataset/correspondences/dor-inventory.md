Notable: the frame hinge is Ravan's wounding by Shankasura and the **amrita** draught (lines 3380-3382; recapped 5749-5754) — the whole Ramayana war is the content of his drugged sleep. The dream narrative itself contains almost no Valmiki episode retellings; the correspondences cluster in the opening editorial/Adhyatma block, Part III's Maricha material, and Part IV's Ghanta Patali backstory.

## Coverage note
